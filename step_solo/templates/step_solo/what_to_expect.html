What to Expect in STEP Solo

STEP Solo is a guided self-care process that helps you work through a recent stressful or upsetting experience. You'll go throug the steps independently, but your STEP Intervention Provider will support you before and after.

What You Have Done So Far:

  1. Consent and Screening

    You have reviewed information about the process, given consent, and completed some short forms. These forms help your STEP Intervention Provider determine whether STEP Solo will be helpful for you.

What's coming up:

  1. Learn Calming Tools

    You will review a document with simple ways to calm your body and mind. You can use these at any time during the process. We will learn more techniques throughout the process to support you in this work.

  2. Watch the Introduction video

    These videos explain how the process works and what materials you'll need. You'll also see how to do bilateral stimulation (tapping and eye movement techniques)that support the intervention.

  3. Grounding Exercise

    You will do the 4 Elements Excercise to feel relaxed and aware. Based on your response, you'll either continue with STEP Solo or be connected with your STEP Intervention Provider to explore other supports that maybe be more helpful for you at this time.

  4. Self-Care and Processing

    You will follow along with videos to:

    • identify a memory that still feels upsetting
    • use guided tapping to reduce stress
    • end with a positive thought and a calming reflection

  5. Close and Follow-Up

    You will complete a final calming exercise and submit your results. Your STEP Intervention Provider will check in with you afterward to see how you're doing and discuss the next steps.


The process should take approximately 90 minutes.


If the information your provide suggests that other interventios may be a better fit for you at this time, you may be guided toward other suports that may be better suited to your current needs. Your STEP Intervention provider will follow up with you to discuss next steps and develop a personalized treatment plan that supports your overall well-being.
